# One floor of stone (z 4) for two worlds: the land of the dead stands on it,
# Proserpina's kingdom hangs from it; turned over, the kingdom stands and the
# land of the dead hangs. Each stone of the floor is walked on from both
# sides, and turning the world over mirrors it across the axle (y 6): what
# lay north lies south. Two seals, one in each world, each raise a stone of
# the floor: a bridge for both worlds, leading somewhere else in each, and
# each seal's bridge is crossed in the other world.
#   - from the start (the north-west crag, its steps hidden from the first
#     view by a spire: turn) to the first pomegranate; a crag cuts the axle
#     above; over: the kingdom's axle is free as far as a marble column;
#   - turned back at the middle pomegranate: the seal of the land of the dead
#     (its steps behind a spire: one view); its stone rises south of the east
#     crag, and from above that way only climbs to a crag (a fragment);
#   - over again at the middle pomegranate: in the kingdom the same stone,
#     mirrored, leads round the marble column to the east end and
#     Proserpina's seal (its stairs behind a tall column: two views); her
#     stone rises north of the third pomegranate, and from her kingdom it only
#     climbs to a terrace (a fragment);
#   - turned back at the third pomegranate: from above it leads to the door,
#     the exit (its steps behind a spire: two views).
