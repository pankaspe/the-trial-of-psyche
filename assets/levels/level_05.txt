# The Trial of Psyche - level II.1: "Cupid's Flight"
# Veiled stones (and planks). Cupid has flown; Psyche has fallen into a forest
# of rock pillars, at the dead of night, far from the palace. A veiled block is
# not there in the dark; the lamp shows it in gold and, close by (about two

#   1. the rope bridge: its planks are veiled; walk it with the lamp lit, each

#      the lamp out, and view 0 joins the bank to it;
#   3. from S1, view 2 joins the hill's ledge (brazier); steps up to the hilltop;
#   4. the hilltop: two veiled caps on two spires. Lit from 7 5 only the first
#      (V2) becomes real, and view 3 joins it; lit from 7 6 (on the way from
#      the steps) the second (W) rises too and covers 7 5 in view 3: the way
#      is lost (back to the brazier);
#   5. from V2, view 2 joins the needle where the seal hides, seen only in the
#      light: lit there, it raises a corridor of rock to the north;
#   6. from the end of the corridor, view 0 joins the clearing under the
#      cypress, where Cupid speaks.
