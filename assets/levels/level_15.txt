# Beyond the river the land of the dead is a floating shelf of rock; under
# it, upside down, hangs Proserpina's kingdom, a palace of pale marble and
# candles. A bridge of dressed stone runs through the middle of both, the
# axle (y 5, z 4); on it grow three of Proserpina's pomegranates. Standing on
# one, the spindle turns the world over about the axle: she stays on her
# stone, now walking on its underside, the other world hanging below.
# The level introduces the flip (the mechanic card):
#   - the crags on the bridge above cut it in three; under it the way is
#     free as far as a pier of marble;
#   - from the start (the mound of the north-west, its stairs hidden from the
#     first view: turn) down to the first pomegranate; over; under the bridge
#     to the second; back up; over the arch of the east crag (its stairs hide
#     behind the crag: turn) to the third; over again, and up to the exit, a
#     door of Proserpina's kingdom on the stair beyond the pier.
