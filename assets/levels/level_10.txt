# The Trial of Psyche - level III.2: "The Flock of the Sun"
# Day and evening (the act's passive mechanic). Venus sends Psyche for the
# golden wool of the Sun's flock; a reed of the river tells her to wait for
# evening. The level starts by day. Stepping onto a reed turns the time: by
# day the sun shows the truth (no illusions, every stair works) and the golden
# rams stand in the way; at evening the illusions are back and the rams lie
# down, each a step up toward its `dir`. A stair under an overhang is never
# seen at evening: a way only by day.
# A crag in a river, too tall for the screen: four bands climbing round the
# core (south, east, north, west), the first three joined by caves; the
# shelves away from the core hang from it, a few blocks thick.
#   the river (h1-h3), the lesson: the first reed brings the evening, the ram
#     lies down, a step up to the ledge; the stair under the overhang needs
#     the day again: the second reed, on a spur; the cave at the top;
#   the east shelf (h7-h8), the ants and the time: a ram (evening) up to the
#     grove, where a seed waits; the ants carry it to the gap of the north
#     walk, but at evening, in the first view, an illusion leads them nearer,
#     to a hollow on the pillar by the river (lost: back to the brazier). By
#     day they go the true way (the reed of the grove), or at evening from
#     another view; the cave at the end of the walk;
#   the turning steps (h12-h15): four steps of rock, each turned by a handle;
#     up, turn the second, down again, turn the first: up to the top. The
#     fourth step (handle at the top) turns toward the needle of the fragment;
#   the flock (h16-h19): a reed and a ram (evening) to the pasture; another
#     ram, the reed on its back brings the day. A seed lies on a ledge: only
#     at evening, from the first view, the ants carry it up across an
#     illusion into the gap of the high terrace, and the gap filled hides the
#     illusion: Psyche goes up by the stair under the overhang (day), onto the
#     reed at its top (evening), across the filled gap and up the last ram to
#     the summit, where the wool is caught on the briars (the exit). Climb
#     before the ants are called and there is no way back (the brazier).
# Solution (solver: 69 steps, 4 turns, 5 handles, 2 ants, 9 times turned, 20
# decisions). Fragments: III.2 (VI.11, Cupid shut away) on the needle at the
# north-west corner of the turning steps: the fourth step turned from the
# top, then the first two set back the other way (11 handles); III.2b (VI.12,
# the reed's advice) on the pillar by the river: the reed of the grove
# stepped on twice, the illusion of the first view at evening.
