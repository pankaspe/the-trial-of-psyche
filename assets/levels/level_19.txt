# The way up (the second diorama, VI.20-21):
#
# The act's last level: the way up out of the underworld, everything of Act IV
# at many heights, so that the dark joins stones across the water. One floor
# (z 4) for both worlds, as in IV.3-IV.5: the land of the dead stands on it,
# Proserpina's kingdom hangs from it, the river lies between its stones.
# Islands: the south bank (the start), three on the axle (a pomegranate each),
# islets in the north-west, north-east and south-east.
#   - Charon's first ferry from the bank to the middle island; over at its
#     pomegranate: her tower (top h7). In the dark, seen from one side (turn)
#     its top joins her stairs' foot; seen from another, it joins the west
#     island: down onto it across the water;
#   - her seal on the west island (its stairs behind a column) raises the steps
#     of the guard's crag in the north-west; back at the west pomegranate, the
#     second ferry (it waited there) to the north-west islet;
#   - the guard candelabrum, lit with the lamp from its crag, shows the stones
#     of a bridge to the north-east ledge (seal 1, they appear in its light);
#   - the third ferry waits at the north-east islet: to the east island; over at
#     its pomegranate, her seal (behind a column) raises two stones of the floor
#     south, a bridge for both worlds; back, across them to the south-east
#     islet, where the seal of the dead raises the steps of the way out;
#   - back to the east island, the third ferry again (it stayed with her), up to
#     the light at the north-east (the exit): she opens the box, the Stygian
#     sleep takes her (`box`).
# Solution (solver: 81 steps, 3 turns, 4 lightings, 4 overturns, 4 crossings,

# of slender pillars, arches over the ways, urns and candelabra. Every piece of
# decoration was kept only if the plan and the fragments' plans stayed the same.
# Fragments: IV.6 (VI.20, the second cake) on a rock floating off the bank,
# reached by an illusion after two turns; IV.6b (VI.21, the light and the
# curiosity) on a rock floating east of the east island, reached by an
# illusion from her floor stones to the south-east, late; IV.6c (VI.20, the
# second coin) on her bridge of veiled planks between the middle and the west
