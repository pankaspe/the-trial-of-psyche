# The Trial of Psyche - level II.5: "The Temple of Juno"
# The last of Act II, a real puzzle with everything learned: turning, hidden
# stairs, the lamp, veiled stones, hidden seals, handles, caves. Dusk going
# into night. The level climbs a rock (vertical), runs right along a ridge
# (horizontal), then goes down into the valley to Juno's temple, where the
# candelabra are lit. The camera follows Psyche (follow).
#   1. the rock: steps cut in its west face, seen only from the west; a cave
#      takes her up from the north ledge to a shoulder over the meadow (a
#      dead end, for now); the north ledge also ends blind, and there, in the
#      lamp's light, a hidden seal: it raises the steps from the shoulder to
#      the summit;
#   2. the ridge: a chasm; the lamp shows a grassy cap on a needle beside it,
#      off the line of the ridge: real once lit near, it is reached in the
#      dark, view 0 from the ridge, and left by view 3 to the far ridge;
#   3. the gate of Juno: an L-shaped bridge turns about its pillar (handles at
#      the gate and on the north balcony) joining two of its doors at a time:
#      the gate, the north balcony, the east terrace; one stone of it is
#      veiled. Its south arm points at nothing: from its tip, view 2 joins the
#      temple's north gallery far below;
#   4. the temple: from the gallery, view 0 joins the top of Juno's column,
#      the handle of the turning stair in the nave; the dark stair between
#      four pillars works only in the light, down to the east ledge, where the
#      second hidden seal raises a stair to a landing over the nave;
#   5. the turning stair has two tasks: turned toward the landing it takes
#      her down to the nave; there a second handle turns it toward the altar
#      (the exit). Turned the wrong way from the column, the landing is a dead
#      end until she goes back up (by view 2 from the risen stair to the
#      bridge's tip, then the gallery and the column).
# Fragments: II.5 on a needle north of the veiled cap (view 1 from the cap);
# II.5b in the grove before the temple, through the east terrace's cave:
# turn the bridge to the north balcony, its handle to the east terrace.
