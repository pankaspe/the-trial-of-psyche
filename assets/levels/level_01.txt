# The Trial of Psyche - level I.1: "Zephyr's Crag" (the prologue)
# Walking, and the first turn. Psyche is left alone on the summit of a crag
# above a sea of clouds, at sunset; a path winds down the crag to its edge.
# The rock where Zephyr waits joins the edge only from one side: turn the crag
# to find it.
# Format: see level_04.txt.

setting crag_sunset
